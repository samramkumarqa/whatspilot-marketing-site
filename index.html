Frequently asked questions

Do I need to change my WhatsApp number?

No. WhatsPilot connects to the WhatsApp Business number you already use — your customers message the same number as always.

What if I don't have a WhatsApp Business number yet?

No problem — we'll walk you through setting one up. It only takes a few minutes to get connected, and you can start chatting with customers the same day.

Will the AI ever say something wrong to a customer?

It only answers from the content you give it. When a question is outside that, or a customer sounds frustrated, WhatsPilot hands the conversation to your team instead of guessing.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes — your team can jump into any conversation, reply manually, and see the same lead and CRM data the AI does.
